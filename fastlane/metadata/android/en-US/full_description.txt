Guandan (掼蛋) is the standard Jiangsu two-deck card game: 108 cards, trump-level cards acting as wildcards, and the four-bomb "heavenly king" combination. Play solo against three AI opponents or over a local network.

Features:
· Nine levels per round with the full promotion/relegation rules
· Three AI tiers: simple strategy, scored decision making, and scored decision plus endgame Monte-Carlo search; thinking depth can be set per tier
· Fully offline, no account, no ads, no analytics
· Five-element (wuxing) visual theme, landscape table, enlarged hand cards
· Built-in Chinese voice announcements for plays and passes, can be switched off
· Play rhythm adjustable in 2/3/5/6 second steps
· Autosave, resume the previous round at any time
· LAN multiplayer: room discovery over UDP broadcast, reconnect support, 22-second move timeout with托管

The interface is in Chinese.
